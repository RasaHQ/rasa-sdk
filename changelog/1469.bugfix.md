The action server no longer crashes at startup with `TypeError: ... argument after ** must be a mapping, not list` when `tracing` in `endpoints.yml` is a list. It accepts a single mapping or a list, ignores `langfuse` entries (Langfuse tracing is handled by the Rasa server), and uses the `jaeger` or `otlp` entry. Configuring more than one `jaeger`/`otlp` backend raises an error, as on the Rasa server.
